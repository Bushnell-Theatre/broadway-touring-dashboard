Model Notes
Fill Rate Projection
Per-performance fill = sold ÷ house capacity. Projected fill = current rate × days-remaining multiplier (60+ days ×1.20 · 31–60 ×1.15 · 15–30 ×1.08 · ≤14 ×1.03), capped at 100%.
Per-Performance Revenue
Each row: capacity × proj. fill × adj. price. Total projected gross = sum of all active performances. Baseline = sold × current price per performance.

Data Source & Methodology

Touring performance data is sourced from the League of American Theatres and Producers weekly grosses report. Data covers multiple touring seasons and includes Broadway-equivalent tours at venues comparable in size to the Bushnell.

Peer venues are classified by house capacity: Size Match venues are within ±15% of Bushnell capacity (~2,200–3,000 seats). Extended includes ±30%.

The days-remaining multiplier reflects historical touring sales curves: shows typically accelerate sales as opening approaches. The multiplier is conservative and does not account for exceptional demand, blockbusters, or adverse external conditions.

All revenue figures are gross (before fees, commissions, or deductions). Top/Mid/Base tier prices should reflect the actual on-sale price points for the Bushnell engagement.
